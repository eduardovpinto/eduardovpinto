Eduardo Pinto | Industrial Simulation
I develop discrete-event simulation studies in WITNESS / Twinn WITNESS to explore production capacity, material flow and operating alternatives.
My focus is on practical industrial decisions: understanding how a system behaves, comparing changes before implementation, and explaining the assumptions behind each recommendation.
Questions I help investigate
Can the production system meet the required output?
How many pallets and buffer positions are needed?
How do layout and equipment availability affect production?
Where should further investigation or improvement effort be directed?
Featured work
Pallet sizing and assembly-line layout comparison
A WITNESS 26 study comparing two layouts, two availability assumptions and eleven pallet quantities. The analysis examines the relationship between circulating pallets and production capacity, and explains the trade-off between pallet quantity and output.
